## Identity

- **Name:** Screenshot Swipe: Smart Notes
- **Tagline:** The smart way to clean up your screenshots
- **Elevator pitch:** Your camera roll is full of screenshots you saved "just in case." Screenshot Swipe turns the pile into something organized, searchable, and clean — swipe right to keep, left to delete, with AI help deciding and on-device OCR making everything findable.
- **Category:** Photo & Video / Utilities
- **Platform:** iPhone, iOS 17.0+, ~15 MB
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/screenshot-swipe-smart-notes/id6757885971 (id 6757885971)
- **Developer:** Michael Rode (solo indie)

## Features (verbatim-verifiable from the listing)

- Swipe triage: right = keep (with optional note/tags/reminder), left = queue for batch deletion (with undo), skip = defer
- Review by date range: this week, this month, or older
- Smart Review (Pro): AI pre-organizes — suggests keep/delete/flag, smart tags, editable notes, items worth flagging
- Notes + tags on any screenshot; AI tagging optional
- Reminders on screenshots that need action
- Tag → Photos album sync
- Full-text search across every kept screenshot (OCR runs on-device)
- Filter by tag, date, status, content; smart collections (Due Soon, Needs Attention, Untagged)
- Native dark mode (3.0 redesign, "Designed for Clarity")
- Works offline (AI features need internet)

## Pricing

- Free: core swipe triage, notes, tags, reminders, on-device OCR search — "no subscription required for the basics"
- Pro: Smart Review AI batch analysis (exact price: check RevenueCat/App Store before quoting)

## Differentiators

1. **Screenshot-specific.** Every competitor (Swipewipe, Slidebox, CleanMy®Phone, Slider) treats screenshots as generic photos. Screenshot Swipe treats them as saved information.
2. **Keep-side organization.** Notes, tags, and reminders attach at the moment of keeping — competitors only do delete-side cleanup.
3. **On-device OCR + full-text search** across kept screenshots — dependable, unlike Live Text's inconsistent coverage.
4. **Privacy-first architecture:** no backend servers, no accounts, no tracking, no ads; photos never leave the device; optional AI sends extracted text only.
5. **AI that drafts, you confirm** — Smart Review suggests, never auto-deletes.

## Honest limitations (use these — they build credibility)

- New app with a tiny rating base (5.0★ from 5 ratings as of Jul 2026) vs. Swipewipe's 82k.
- Screenshots only — does nothing for a camera roll full of photos/videos.
- No duplicate finder, no storage-reclaim dashboard.
- Smart Review requires internet and a Pro purchase.
- iPhone only; no iPad/Android.

## Brand voice

- Same editorial rules as GainFrame (authority first, sell at most twice, no antithesis constructions, no meta headers, no emoji).
- The recurring narrative: screenshots are **saved information, not photos** — cleanup tools should preserve the information, not just delete pixels. (Express this positively; don't phrase it as "not X, but Y" in published copy.)
- Privacy claims are load-bearing and verifiable in the privacy policy — never overstate them (AI features DO send extracted text to Google's Gemini API when enabled).
