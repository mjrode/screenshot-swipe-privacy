Clean up your screenshot pile, swipe by swipe

Your camera roll is full of screenshots you saved "just in case" — receipts, recipes, that thing your friend texted you. Screenshot Swipe turns the pile into something organized, searchable, and clean.

How it works

Review a week of screenshots in under two minutes.

Swipe right to keep

Save it with an optional note, tags, or a reminder so you remember why it mattered.

Swipe left to delete

Queue junk for batch deletion — with undo if your thumb gets ahead of you.

Skip for later

On the fence? Defer it. Review by date range: this week, this month, or older.

Find anything, fast

Everything you keep becomes searchable and organized.

Full-text search

OCR runs on-device, so you can search the text inside every screenshot you keep.

Smart Review (Pro)

AI pre-sorts your pile — suggesting what to keep, delete, or flag, with tags and notes you can edit.

Notes, tags & reminders

Tag screenshots, sync tags to Photos albums, and set reminders so nothing important gets lost.

Privacy first

Photos and OCR stay on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no ads. AI features are optional.

Your screenshots stay yours

Screenshot Swipe has no backend servers. Photo processing and text extraction happen entirely on your iPhone, and the optional AI features send only extracted text — never your images.
