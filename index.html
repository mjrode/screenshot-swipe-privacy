Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 21, 2026

Screenshot Swipe operates primarily on your device. Optional AI features may send extracted text to Google's servers.

Overview

Screenshot Swipe is designed with privacy as a priority. We do not collect or store any personal data on our servers. The App does not have its own backend servers.

Photo Library Access

The app requests access to your photo library to:

  • Display your screenshots for review
  • Delete screenshots you choose to remove
  • Keep track of screenshots you've saved

All photo processing happens locally on your device. Your actual images never leave your device.

AI-Powered Features (Optional)

The app includes optional AI features ("Smart Analysis") that use Google's Gemini API to generate summaries and suggest tags.

You can disable AI features at any time in Settings → Smart Analysis.

What data is sent

When you tap "Scan & Analyze" with Smart Analysis enabled:

  • Text extracted from your screenshot is sent to Google's Gemini API
  • Only the text content is sent, not the actual image
  • Your tags list is sent to help with tag suggestions

What data is NOT sent

  • Your actual screenshot images
  • Your personal information
  • Your notes or reminders
  • Any data from screenshots you don't actively scan

When disabled

When Smart Analysis is disabled:

  • No data is sent to external servers
  • Text extraction (OCR) still works entirely on-device
  • Tag suggestions use local keyword matching only

When using AI features, your data is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

On-Device Text Extraction

The app uses Apple's Vision framework to extract text from screenshots. This processing happens entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications for reminders you set. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device.

Data Storage

Any data the app stores (such as tags, notes, and saved screenshot references) is kept locally on your device using Apple's standard data storage frameworks.

Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Shared Optional
Google Gemini API AI text analysis Extracted text Yes

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
