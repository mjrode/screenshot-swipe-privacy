Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 14, 2026

Screenshot Swipe does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Overview

Screenshot Swipe is designed with privacy as a priority. The app operates entirely on your device with no data transmitted to external servers.

Photo Library Access

The app requests access to your photo library to:

  • Display your screenshots for review
  • Delete screenshots you choose to remove
  • Keep track of screenshots you've saved

All photo processing happens locally on your device. Your photos never leave your device through this app.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications for reminders you set. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers.

Data Storage

Any data the app stores (such as tags, notes, and saved screenshot references) is kept locally on your device using Apple's standard data storage frameworks. This data is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

Screenshot Swipe does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
